# V19 — Trade Reflection Engine

V19 is intentionally a behind-the-scenes brain release.

Every validated completed trade is reviewed on separate dimensions:
- was the execution/risk process correct?
- what was the earliest observable clue that the thesis was failing?
- would the opposite direction have performed materially better over the same replay?
- what clues may have been missed?
- has that clue repeated often enough to deserve formal review?

A stopped-out loser can be graded `GOOD PROCESS / WRONG DIRECTION`. This explicitly praises disciplined capital protection while still investigating the market-reading mistake.

Reverse-trade analysis is hindsight research only and cannot rewrite the original decision. Reflection-derived lessons require repeated evidence and formal/out-of-sample review before any live-rule change.
