# V17 — Time Intelligence + Learning Evidence Centre

V17 makes time a first-class part of the AI's learning.

Every validated completed trade can now record:
- entry timestamp
- exit timestamp
- total holding duration
- time to first profitable observation
- time to MFE
- time to MAE
- share of observed holding period spent profitable
- time from peak to actual exit
- exit-to-re-entry delay when re-entry evidence exists

Move Phase Intelligence classifies chronological market behaviour as:
IGNITION → ACCELERATION → TRENDING → EXTENSION → EXHAUSTION → RESET / REVERSAL.

These states describe timed momentum behaviour. They do not identify market manipulation and never justify blindly chasing price.

The new Learning Evidence Centre is the audit layer. Each learning claim shows its source brain, sample count, sample gate, supporting examples, counter-examples and whether it is still testing or eligible for formal use.

Historical future information is permitted for post-trade labels only. It is explicitly excluded from live decision inputs.
