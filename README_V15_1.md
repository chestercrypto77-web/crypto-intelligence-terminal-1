# V15.1 — Strategy Brain Control Centre

V15.1 redesigns Strategy Lab around learning progress rather than a misleading tiny-sample leaderboard.

## Front-line changes
- Champion is clearly identified as the baseline.
- Challengers show what they are testing in plain English.
- `0 completed trades` displays no win-rate claim.
- Evidence maturity is shown as progress toward 30 completed trades.
- Open paper equity is kept separate from completed strategy evidence.
- Every strategy gets a current plain-English conclusion.
- A Current Discoveries section summarises only evidence that actually exists.
- Connected Brain Status shows what Winner School, Failure School, Trade Integrity, Profit Capture, Market School, Microstructure, Pattern Miner, Management Challengers, Committee Memory and AI Scorecard are doing and how mature their evidence is.

## Behind the scenes
Each strategy now accumulates market snapshots, signals checked, filters applied, entries opened and completed trades. Strategy Brain Status consolidates the separate learning engines and its evidence-based discoveries feed the existing cross-learning bus.

No small sample automatically promotes a strategy. The current Champion remains the baseline until a challenger earns a formal review with sufficient completed evidence.
